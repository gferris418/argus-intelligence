# ARGUS 3.0.0-rc.1

**Status:** Candidate  
**Release date:** 2026-10-22

## Summary

ARGUS 3.0.0-rc.1 redefines the product as ATLAS-governed **government commercial opportunity intelligence** for Access Asia. Tender and procurement intelligence remain important, but no longer define the product boundary. The system now treats the commercial opportunity as the central object and works across the full government need-to-delivery lifecycle while building international supplier/OEM intelligence in parallel.

## Major architecture changes

- Five persistent engines: Demand Intelligence, Supply Intelligence, Opportunity Engine, Matching & Qualification, and Early Warning.
- First-class pre-PX Opportunity identity: `ARGUS-OPP-YYYY-NNNNNN`.
- Access Asia PX identity is assigned only after ATLAS-authorized promotion and remains linked to the permanent ARGUS identity.
- S0–S9 lifecycle replaces I0–I9 as the primary government-opportunity lifecycle model.
- Commercial modes are separate from stage: `SHAPE`, `POSITION`, `REACT`, `EXPAND`.
- Bidirectional discovery supports both government-need → supplier and OEM/technology → government-need paths.
- Supplier intelligence now includes partnerability, existing representation/conflicts, channel model, willingness to partner, local support and Access Asia relationship context.
- Opportunity–supplier matching supports configurable weighting by default, sector and opportunity.
- ARGUS computes advisory opportunity qualification; ATLAS retains authoritative priority, PX promotion, pursuit, bid/no-bid and external-action authority.
- Event-driven early warning tracks policy, feasibility, project, budget, approval, procurement and delivery/expansion changes against one persistent opportunity.
- Rapid Tender Mode provides an accelerated recovery path when first material discovery occurs at live-tender stage.
- Detection lag and early-detection KPIs make ARGUS intelligence performance measurable.

## Evidence and probability semantics

ARGUS now explicitly separates:

1. evidence confidence;
2. procurement realization probability;
3. Access Asia success probability; and
4. supplier-match confidence.

These measures are never interchangeable.

## Procurement-integrity boundary

ARGUS may support legitimate technical education, demonstrations, concept notes, transparent pre-procurement engagement, industry consultation and solution discovery. It explicitly prohibits bribery/inducements, collusion, improper procurement influence, unlawful specification manipulation, confidential bid-information acquisition, procurement-evaluation interference and misrepresentation of government support.

## Compatibility

The ATLAS activation boundary is unchanged:

- authority: ATLAS OS;
- gate: Collection-Need Gate;
- modes: `ARGUS_COLLECTION`, `HYBRID`;
- capability: `intelligence_collection`;
- activation contract version: `3.0.0`.

Legacy I0–I9 semantics remain compatibility-only for historical/public-sector records. Existing Bangladesh procurement and evidence contracts remain preserved.

## Validation

Candidate validation covers:

- first-class opportunity/supplier/event/match schemas;
- opportunity and supplier-match scoring;
- detection-lag calculation;
- expanded collection mission types;
- Access Asia identity/lifecycle preservation;
- full existing ARGUS contract suite; and
- the real ATLAS→ARGUS→ATLAS cross-repository producer/consumer/return path.

## Follow-on ATLAS integration

ATLAS must advance its current candidate to pin ARGUS 3.0.0-rc.1, recognize the expanded collection mission types and expose the revised ARGUS commercial-opportunity semantics while preserving the same Collection-Need activation boundary and final decision authority.
